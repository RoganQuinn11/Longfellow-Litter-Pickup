About

Hi! I’m Rogan Quinn, a proud resident of the Longfellow neighborhood in Minneapolis. I started picking up litter on my daily walks in 2025, and what started as a solo effort has grown into a community movement. I believe small actions add up to big change—and I’m inviting you to join me.
